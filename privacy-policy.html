Privacy Policy

App: AdRewards • Package: com.bhavin.adrewards • Effective: October 26, 2026
Summary: We respect your privacy. AdRewards uses secure Google Authentication to identify you, tracks rewarded ad completions, and collects payout addresses only to deliver your earnings. We never sell your personal data to data brokers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines the practices of AdRewards (“we”, “us”, or “our”) concerning the collection, storage, processing, and disclosure of information gathered through our mobile application available on the Google Play Store (Package ID: com.bhavin.adrewards) and associated web services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to provide a fair, reliable, and anti-fraud rewarded experience:

A. Personal Information (via Google Authentication)

When you register or log in using Google Auth via Firebase, we receive:

  • Google Display Name: Used to personalize your greeting and app profile.
  • Email Address: Used as your unique account credential, passwordless recovery, and payout confirmation.
  • Google Profile Picture URL: Displayed in your in-app profile header.
  • Google User ID (UID): A unique pseudonymized token to link your cloud balance.

B. Device & Technical Identifiers

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID): Standardized anonymous identifier provided by Google Play Services to serve and verify rewarded ad impressions.
  • Hardware / Device Fingerprint: Used solely for our Anti-Fraud Shield to prevent emulator scripts, bot farms, and multiple accounts from claiming duplicate referral rewards.
  • IP Address & Country: Used for geographic ad matching and anti-fraud validation.

C. Financial & Payout Details

When submitting a withdrawal request for your earnings, you may provide your UPI ID, Paytm mobile number, PayPal email, or cryptocurrency address. This information is stored with encryption and used solely to disburse your cashout.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Our application integrates trusted third-party services that process data in strict accordance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Provides Firebase Authentication, Crashlytics, and session token security.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Required for Google Auth, Google Play billing/codes, and security checks.
  • Supabase Inc: Cloud Postgres database hosting user balances and payout requests with Row Level Security (RLS).
    Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Rewarded Ad Mediation Networks: Deliver third-party sponsored video ads. These SDKs may collect anonymous advertising IDs to prevent repeat ads and measure completion rates.

4. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

AdRewards is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from minors. If we discover that a minor under 13 has registered, we will promptly delete their account and associated data.

5. Data Retention & Security Measures

All communication between the mobile app, Firebase, and Supabase is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3/HTTPS). Your account data is retained for as long as your account remains active. If your account is inactive for over 12 consecutive months or upon receiving a deletion request, your data is scrubbed.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

Under applicable laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we store about you.
  • Request correction or rectification of any inaccurate information.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.

To request deletion, you can tap “Delete My Account” inside the mobile app or visit our public deletion portal:
👉 User Data Deletion Request Page
