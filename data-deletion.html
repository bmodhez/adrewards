Account & User Data Deletion

Google Play Store Compliance Policy • Application: AdRewards (com.bhavin.adrewards)
Google Play Data Safety Mandate: In accordance with Google Play developer program guidelines, AdRewards provides full user control over personal data. You have the right to permanently erase your account and all associated cloud data.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account using any of the three options below:

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the AdRewards app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the 🛡️ (Shield) icon in the top header, or tap Data Deletion in the bottom footer.
  3. Select the Data Deletion tab.
  4. Tap the red button: “🗑️ Permanently Delete My Account & Data”.
  5. Confirm the dialog. Your session will be terminated and your data erased immediately.

Option 2: Direct Email Request

Send an email from the Google email address associated with your AdRewards account to:
📧 juliemodhh@gmail.com
Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request
Please include your Google Account email. Your request will be fulfilled within 24 to 48 hours.

Option 3: Online Web Deletion Ticket

Submit your deletion request through the web form below:

✅ Your data deletion ticket has been recorded. Your account and stored records will be permanently expunged within 24 business hours. Confirmation will be sent to your email.

What Data is Deleted?

When your account is deleted, the following items are permanently removed from our active servers:

  • Google Identity Data: Your Google User ID (UID), email address, display name, and avatar URL.
  • Financial Records: Available balance, accumulated streak counters, daily challenge progress, and pending reward tickets.
  • Device Identifiers: Anonymous hardware fingerprint hashes and advertising session logs.

What Data is Retained (if any)?

Completed withdrawal payout disbursement transaction IDs are archived in encrypted offline logs for up to 90 days solely to comply with banking, tax, and anti-money laundering fraud regulations, after which they are permanently destroyed.
