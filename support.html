Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I earn rewards in the app?
A: Tap “WATCH AD TO EARN” on the home screen. Watch the 10-second sponsored video until the reward countdown completes, then tap Claim. You can also claim daily check-in streak rewards, complete 5 daily missions, and invite friends.
Q: What is the minimum withdrawal threshold?
A: The minimum threshold to cash out is $20.00 USD (or local currency equivalent, such as ₹1,730 INR, €18.40 EUR, or £15.60 GBP). Once your available balance hits $20.00, the “WITHDRAW FUNDS” button activates automatically.
Q: Which payout methods are supported?
A: We support UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm UPI), Paytm Wallet mobile transfer, PayPal email transfer, USDT (Crypto TRC20/BEP20), and Google Play redeem codes.
Q: How long do withdrawals take to arrive?
A: Withdrawal requests undergo an automated security audit to verify genuine ad impressions and are typically processed within 3 to 5 business days.
Q: What is the 7-Day Login Streak?
A: Check in once every 24 hours to keep your streak alive. An active streak gives you a 1.2x earning boost on all rewarded ads, and completing Day 7 unlocks the $1.20 Grand Mystery Jackpot!
Q: Why does it say “Daily Limit Reached”?
A: To prevent automated bot abuse and maintain premium ad payouts from advertisers, each user has a fair daily limit of $1.00. The counter resets automatically every night at 00:00 midnight local time.
Q: How does the Anti-Fraud Referral Shield work?
A: Each user receives a unique referral code. When your friend signs up and enters your code, they receive a +$0.50 welcome gift and you earn +$0.70. Our system prevents self-referrals and locks to physical hardware IDs to block clone farms.
Q: How can I delete my account and data?
A: You can delete your account inside the app via the 🛡️ Legal menu, or visit our Data Deletion Request Page.
